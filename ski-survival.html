The rescues

Every wipeout summons something ridiculous

Most games punish a crash. This one answers it. Go down hard and the mountain sends help, and help is having a strange day.

You do not pick which one arrives. That is the point. Players compare notes about which ones they have seen, and there are ones you have not seen yet.

The run

Distance is the goal. Flow is the reason to take the bad line.

Each level gives you a target and a rescue line to clear. Then the hill starts offering reasons to leave your line. Coins where you should not be. An animal that needs help. A UFO with a tractor beam and a countdown, which a thrown pole can settle if your timing is right.

Keep it clean and the flow multiplier climbs. Take the greedy line and it can climb faster, right up until it does not.

Easy mode is not the same hill made slower. It is a hill built so a kid and the person who taught them can both finish a run and mean it.

  • Flow multiplierRide clean and it builds. It is the difference between a good run and a story.
  • Speed wax and scrubGo faster, or dump speed before the thing you did not see coming.
  • Jetpack burstHeld items that change what a run is for. Some help you go. Some help you save.
  • Flip and diveAir is scored. Landing it is optional and heavily encouraged.
  • Touch and controllerThe same game on a phone, a desktop, or a handheld. No keyboard required.
The roster

Four skiers. One of them is not a person.

Each rider handles differently, and the Yeti has to be earned. He is heavier, he carries a first aid kit, and he clears things the others should not attempt.

Privacy Policy for Ski Survival

Ski Survival does not collect, store, share, or sell personal information.

The app does not require an account. The app does not collect names, email addresses, location data, contacts, photos, health data, financial information, or user-generated content.

The app does not use third-party advertising or tracking. The app does not track users across apps or websites.

Ski Survival is a simple offline game. Any gameplay progress or settings are stored locally on the user's device only. The My Music feature does not access or inspect the player's music library. Audio from outside the game is managed by iOS.
